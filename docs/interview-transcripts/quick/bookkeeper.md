## Questions

### 1. Tell me about your business: what do you do for your clients, and who are they?

_Why:_ Knowing who sends the receipts and what you do with them shapes everything else.

**Answer:** I'm a self-employed bookkeeper with one part-time assistant. I look after about 40 small businesses, mostly tradespeople, a few shops, and two cafés, entering their receipts and statements into their accounting software and sending them a short monthly report.

_3.7s, $0.014_

### 2. How do clients get you their receipts and statements today, and what happens to them once they arrive?

_Why:_ Knowing where receipts pile up today shows what the app has to take over.

_Choices:_ Photos by text or WhatsApp / Email / Paper handed over or posted / Bank statements downloaded by them / Shared folder

**Answer:** Photos by text or WhatsApp, Email, Paper handed over or posted

_4.8s, $0.017; builds on an answer_

### 3. Walk me through a typical month for one client: when do their receipts arrive, and how do you find out something is missing?

_Why:_ Chasing missing receipts is often where a bookkeeper's time goes, and it shapes what clients need to see.

**Answer:** Their receipts and bank statements usually come in during the month by email, WhatsApp, or as a shoebox of paper, and often a lot of it lands at the end of the quarter. I check my spreadsheet of what each client has sent this month, and if something's missing I chase them by email.

_4.7s, $0.016; raises late payers_

### 4. What goes wrong or eats the most time today when you chase a client for missing receipts?

_Why:_ Knowing the biggest headache tells me what the app must fix first.

_Choices:_ Clients ignore or are slow to answer my chasing / I can't tell what is missing until I sort everything / Receipts are unreadable or arrive in the wrong place / The quarter-end rush piles up all at once

**Answer:** I can't tell what is missing until I sort everything

_4.7s, $0.017_

### 5. What is the last step of a month for one client: after you enter everything, how does the monthly report reach them and does anything get paid or signed off?

_Why:_ Knowing how a month ends tells me what the client needs to see and when a client counts as done.

_Choices:_ I email the report and that's it / I email the report and they must approve it / I send the report and then invoice them / I send the report, they approve, then I invoice

**Answer:** I email the report and that's it

_4.9s, $0.017_

### 6. On a Monday morning, what would you most want to see at a glance across your 40 clients?

_Why:_ You said you can't tell what's missing until you've sorted everything, so I want the first screen to answer that straight away.

_Choices:_ Which clients have sent nothing this month / What each client is still missing / New items that arrived and aren't entered yet / Which clients are late for quarter end / Who I've already chased and when

**Answer:** What each client is still missing

_6.2s, $0.020_

### 7. How do you work out that something is missing for a client?

_Why:_ The app can only show what is missing if it knows what you expect from each client.

_Choices:_ A bank statement line has no receipt / A fixed checklist each month (statements, takings, invoices) / I know from experience what each client usually sends / A mix of these

**Answer:** A fixed checklist each month (statements, takings, invoices)

_6.1s, $0.021_

### 8. Which of these should the app do from the first day, with the rest staying as they are today?

_Why:_ You chase by email and email the report now, so I want to know which of those you'd hand over first.

_Choices:_ Clients send receipts and statements in / Tick off the monthly checklist for each client / Send clients a reminder about what's missing / Send the monthly report / Enter receipts into the accounting software

**Answer:** Clients send receipts and statements in, Tick off the monthly checklist for each client, Send clients a reminder about what's missing

_6.7s, $0.022_

### 9. You said every client sends statements, takings and invoices. Do some clients differ, such as cafés and shops with daily takings, or tradespeople with none?

_Why:_ If clients' checklists differ, one shared list would show false 'missing' items and bury the real ones.

_Choices:_ Everyone has exactly the same checklist / Each client has a slightly different checklist / Only a few clients differ

**Answer:** Each client has a slightly different checklist

_7.4s, $0.023; builds on an answer_

### 10. Which of these has caused you real trouble when handling clients' receipts?

_Why:_ Knowing what costs you money or time when it goes wrong tells me what the app must never let happen.

_Choices:_ A blurry photo I can't read / A receipt filed under the wrong client or month / A client says they sent something I never saw / Items arriving too late at quarter end

**Answer:** A receipt filed under the wrong client or month, Items arriving too late at quarter end

_8.0s, $0.024_

## The plan at the end

- business: Clients send their receipts to the business and see what the business still needs from them.
- business: A self-employed bookkeeper with one part-time assistant looks after about 40 small clients, mostly tradespeople, a few shops and two cafés.
- business: She enters each client's receipts and statements into their accounting software and sends a short monthly report.
- who: Clients send receipts and see what is still needed from them.
- who: The bookkeeper and her part-time assistant handle the receipts.
- replaces: Today receipts arrive as photos by text or WhatsApp, by email, and as paper handed over or posted.
- replaces: She keeps a spreadsheet of what each client has sent this month and chases missing items by email.
- business: Receipts and bank statements arrive through the month, with a big rush at the end of each quarter.
- business: Biggest pain: she cannot tell what is missing for a client until she has sorted everything that arrived.
- business: She emails each client a monthly report; nothing is paid or signed off through the app.
- does: Clients send receipt photos and statements to one place instead of text, WhatsApp, email and paper.
- does: One overview shows every client and what each is still missing, for Monday mornings.
- does: Each client sees their own list of what is still needed from them.
- rules: Each client has their own monthly checklist (for example statements, takings, invoices), set up by the bookkeeper and different from client to client.
- does: The app shows each client's monthly checklist and what has arrived against it, so missing items show without sorting.
- does: The bookkeeper can send a client a reminder listing what is still missing from their checklist.
- does: Entering receipts into accounting software and emailing the monthly report stay as they are today. (later)
- rules: The bookkeeper or her assistant can change a client's checklist at any time.
- rules: A receipt must never be filed under the wrong client or month; every upload is tied to one client and one month.
- rules: Near quarter end, clients with items still missing stand out in the overview so late items are chased early.
