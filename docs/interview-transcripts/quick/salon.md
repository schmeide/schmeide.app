## Questions

### 1. In a few words, what does Studio Lumen sell, and who are your clients?

_Why:_ Knowing what an appointment is for tells me how booking should work for you.

**Answer:** We sell hair services: women's and men's cuts, color, highlights and blow-dries. Our clients are mostly regulars from the neighborhood, and some come in every six weeks.

_Took:_ 3.9s, $0.014

### 2. Today, how does a client get an appointment with you, from first asking to paying after the service?

_Why:_ Knowing how it works now shows what the online booking has to take over.

**Answer:** Clients phone or message me on Instagram to ask for a slot, and I write it in the big paper book at the desk. They pay at the till by card after the appointment.

_Took:_ 4.3s, $0.015

### 3. How many people cut and color hair at Studio Lumen, and roughly how many appointments do you have in a normal week?

_Why:_ The number of stylists and appointments decides how the booking calendar has to work.

**Answer:** Three of us cut and color: me, Mia and Joe. Our junior only washes hair. We get about 120 appointments a week.

_Took:_ 4.6s, $0.016

### 4. What goes wrong most with the paper book and phone or Instagram bookings today?

_Why:_ Knowing what costs you time or money tells me what the online booking must get right first.

_Choices:_ Double bookings or clashes / Clients not turning up / Time lost answering calls and messages / Wrong length of slot for color or highlights / Hard to see who is free when

**Answer:** Double bookings or clashes

_Took:_ 4.0s, $0.017

### 5. When a clash happens, what usually causes it?

_Why:_ Knowing the cause tells me what the booking has to guard against, such as a slot taken by phone and Instagram at once.

_Choices:_ Phone and Instagram bookings for the same slot / Different appointment lengths for color and cuts / Someone writing in the wrong stylist's column / Changes or cancellations not updated in the book

**Answer:** Phone and Instagram bookings for the same slot

_Took:_ 4.4s, $0.017

### 6. What would save you the most time each week, and what do you want to see at a glance on a Monday morning?

_Why:_ Knowing what you check first each week shows what the app should put front and center.

_Choices:_ Each stylist's week and open slots / Who is booked in today / Gaps and cancellations to fill / Which regulars are due back

**Answer:** Each stylist's week and open slots

_Took:_ 4.4s, $0.017

### 7. When a client books online, how should they choose their slot?

_Why:_ Your clients are regulars who often return every six weeks, so how they pick a stylist and time decides how the booking works.

_Choices:_ Pick a stylist, then see that stylist's open times / Pick a time, and any free stylist is assigned / Both ways should work

**Answer:** Both ways should work

_Took:_ 4.5s, $0.018

### 8. Besides clients booking online, which of these steps should the app handle from the first day?

_Why:_ Phone and Instagram bookings caused your clashes, so where they get written down decides whether double bookings really stop.

_Choices:_ You and staff enter phone and Instagram bookings in the same calendar / Reminders sent to clients before their appointment / Prompting regulars to rebook around six weeks / Taking card payment online

**Answer:** You and staff enter phone and Instagram bookings in the same calendar, Reminders sent to clients before their appointment

_Took:_ 6.3s, $0.020

### 9. You take card payment at the till after the appointment. Should that stay as it is, or should the app take anything online?

_Why:_ Deposits can cut no-shows, but they add work, so it's worth choosing on purpose.

_Choices:_ Payment stays at the till / Clients pay a deposit online when booking / Clients pay in full online when booking

**Answer:** Clients pay a deposit online when booking

_Took:_ 10.7s, $0.041; builds on an answer; raises no-shows; raises deposits and part-payments

### 10. If a client cancels or doesn't turn up after paying a deposit, what should happen to the deposit?

_Why:_ Salons that take deposits lose money or argue with regulars when this isn't clear from the start.

_Choices:_ Refunded if they cancel 24 hours ahead, otherwise kept / Always kept if they cancel or don't show / Always refunded on cancel, kept only for no-shows / I decide case by case

**Answer:** Refunded if they cancel 24 hours ahead, otherwise kept

_Took:_ 7.2s, $0.023; raises no-shows; raises cancellation rules
